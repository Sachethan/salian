Adventure Desk

Water Sports Season

Jet Ski, Speed Boats and Bumper Rides run 8:30 AM – 6:00 PM from October to May. Rates are set at the counter on the day; ask for group packages. Motorized sports pause during the monsoon (Jun–Sep).

Visitor Compliance

Blue Flag Rules vs Tourist Myths

Unlike Malpe or Panambur, Padubidri is governed by strict FEE (Denmark) criteria. Know what is prohibited before you arrive.

✕ Strictly Prohibited

  • Zero single-use plastic: bags, chip packets and disposable bottles are confiscated at the scanner.
  • No alcohol or smoking: zero tolerance, heavy fines.
  • No domestic pets on the certified sand perimeter.
  • No night camping or bonfires: park closes after sunset.

✓ Included With ₹40 Entry

  • Sanitized showers & changing rooms — free freshwater rinse stations.
  • Purified drinking water kiosks — bring your own bottle.
  • Wheelchair & stroller ramps right up to the water's edge.
  • Professional lifeguards with motorized rescue capability.

Visitor Guidelines

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the photography and pre-wedding shoot rules?

Casual mobile photography is free. Commercial productions, drone videography and pre-wedding shoots need a nominal sanitation token and clearance at the main gate reception.

Are pets allowed on the Blue Flag beach?

No. Under Blue Flag hygiene and sand purity rules, domestic pets are restricted from the certified swimming shoreline.

Do water sports run during the monsoon?

Motorized water sports and open-sea swimming are suspended in the high monsoon (typically June–September). Kayaking in protected backwater channels continues when calm.
