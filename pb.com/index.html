Outsider Transit Intelligence

How to Reach Without Getting Overcharged

Most outsiders get stuck with return cab cancellations or inflated auto fares. Here is the verified local playbook for transit along the Mangalore–Udupi highway belt.

NH 66 Express Buses (Direct Drop-off)

Take any Express or Service bus running between Mangalore (State Bank / KSRTC) and Udupi / Manipal (every 5–10 mins). Ask conductor for "Padubidri Junction".
Fare: ₹40–₹55

⚠️ Tourist Cab Warning (Uber / Ola)

Ola and Uber cabs drop off at Padubidri from Mangalore/Airport, but you cannot book return rides via app from the beach.
Padubidri Taxi Stand: +91 8252 250000 Tip: Hire for 2-way waiting or take auto back to NH 66.

Visitor Compliance Guidelines • Rules & Entry Fees

Unlike Malpe or Panambur, Padubidri is an international eco-reserve governed by strict FEE (Denmark) criteria. Know what is prohibited before arriving.

Strictly Prohibited on Blue Flag Sand

  • ✕ Zero Single-Use Plastic: Polythene bags, chips packets, and disposable plastic bottles are confiscated at the security scanner.
  • ✕ No Alcohol or Smoking: Strictly zero-tolerance policy. Heavy fines and police deployment active across the park.
  • ✕ No Domestic Pets: Dogs and pets are banned on the certified sand perimeter to preserve microbiological water testing scores.
  • ✕ No Night Camping or Bonfires: Park closes promptly after sunset; no unauthorized overnight stay permitted.

Included with Your Entry Token (₹40)

  • ✓ Sanitized Showers & Changing Rooms: Free access to modern, freshwater rinse stations and changing booths.
  • ✓ Purified Drinking Water Kiosks: Free chilled RO water refill stations (bring your reusable bottle!).
  • ✓ Wheelchair & Stroller Accessibility: Continuous solid ramp network leading right up to the water’s edge.
  • ✓ Professional Lifeguard Protection: Dedicated surveillance tower with motorized rescue capability.

Official Tourism & Environmental Benchmark

Blue Flag Certification & Eco-Sanctuary Standards

Padubidri Beach was certified under the prestigious international Blue Flag programme by Denmark-based Foundation for Environmental Education (FEE). The certification requires rigorous compliance with 33 criteria across four core categories: Environmental Education, Water Quality, Environmental Management, and Safety & Services.

Microbial Water Purity

Bi-weekly sampling verifies zero industrial runoff and strict microbiological swimming safety standards.

Greywater Recycling

All washroom and rinse water passes through an on-site bio-treatment plant to irrigate beachfront palm lawns.

Lifeguard Towers

Staffed daily by certified rescue lifeguards equipped with motorized craft, buoy lines, and first-aid kits.

Wheelchair Accessibility

A solid ramp system and elevated wooden boardwalk allow unrestricted access for wheelchairs and strollers.

Visitor Guidelines • Frequently Asked Questions

What are the photography and pre-wedding shoot rules?

Casual mobile photography is completely free. Commercial productions, drone videography, and pre-wedding photo shoots require a nominal sanitation token and clearance at the main gate reception counter.

Are pets allowed on the Blue Flag certified beach?

In strict accordance with international Blue Flag hygiene and sand purity mandates, domestic pets are restricted from the primary certified swimming shoreline to preserve water quality.

Do water sports operate during the monsoon season?

For passenger safety, motorized water sports and open-sea swimming are suspended during the high monsoon period (typically June through September). Kayaking in protected backwater channels continues when calm.
