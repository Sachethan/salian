Everything You Need to Know About Padubidri Beach

Where River Meets Sea & Sunset Glows

Padubidri Beach in Udupi district, Karnataka, is renowned for its tranquil golden shorelines and the dramatic natural confluence where the Kamini river embraces the Arabian Sea at the southern Padubidri End Point. Unlike commercialized beaches, it maintains quiet dignity and pristine cleanliness.

Why Padubidri Beach is Unique:

  • ✓ International Eco-Standard: One of very few beaches in India holding the prized Blue Flag certification for eco-sustainability and cleanliness.
  • ✓ Family & Child Safety: Complete enclosure, round-the-clock trained lifeguards, watch towers, and dedicated shallow swimming zones.
  • ✓ Padubidri End Point: Stroll just 1.5 km to where the gentle backwaters blend into the open ocean, an iconic photography spot.

Coastal Delicacies & Local Flavors

Right outside the eco-boundary, enjoy genuine Tulunadu seafood and vegetarian culinary wonders: Neer Dosa, freshly caught Anjal (King Fish) tawa fry, Kori Rotti, Tender Coconuts, and authentic Mangalore buns.

How to Reach Padubidri

By Road: Located adjacent to National Highway 66 (NH 66). Frequent buses operate between Mangaluru and Udupi, dropping passengers right at Padubidri bus station (auto rickshaws are available 2 km directly to the beach).

By Rail: The nearest Konkan Railway stations are Padubidri Railway Station (3 km) and Mulki Railway Station (11 km).

By Air: Mangaluru International Airport (IXE) is just 35 km away with taxi services directly to Padubidri Beach.
